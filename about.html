Bio

I'm Abhisek Mishra, an AI/ML engineer. I started in classic data science — building prediction models, classifiers, and automated scraping/OCR pipelines — and have since moved into Generative AI, building LLM-powered applications like RAG systems and automated content tools.

During my time as a Data Science Intern at Fightright Technologies, I built scraping pipelines with Selenium, stored results in Amazon S3, and designed OCR automation using AWS Lambda, Docker, and Amazon ECR to process image-based legal documents. I also worked hands-on with MySQL for CRUD operations, data cleaning, and validation.

That foundation now underpins my GenAI work — I build with LangChain, vector databases, and LLM APIs to ship practical tools: multi-document RAG chatbots, automated video summarizers, and the ML models that often sit underneath them.

Outside of code, I enjoy music, traveling, and staying curious about emerging tech.
